About & Data Accuracy

This application ships with sample data only, clearly labelled, for testing the workflow. No facility names, phone numbers, addresses, coordinates, doctors, purchasing managers or suppliers have been verified. Before real-world use, import a verified facility database — the UAE Ministry of Health & Prevention (MOHAP) medical facilities directory is the recommended primary source, and provides filters by facility type, specialty and emirate. Fields with no verified value are shown as "Not available" rather than invented.

The Analytics → Sharjah Market Size table uses real official counts (not names) from MOHAP's open-data "Number of Health Facilities" dataset for Sharjah, 2024 — see Analytics for the full breakdown and source note.
